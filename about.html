About The Krusty Krab

Home of the legendary Krabby Patty, The Krusty Krab has been serving delicious food and creating unforgettable memories for decades.

Founded by the shrewd and hardworking Mr. Krabs, our restaurant is known for its secret formula and welcoming atmosphere. From Bikini Bottom locals to curious tourists, everyone is invited to pull up a seat.
